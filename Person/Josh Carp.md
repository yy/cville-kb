**Josh Carp** (Joshua Carp) is a software engineer and Charlottesville resident who builds civic data tools about the city under the Cville Data project (cvilledata.org). He briefly ran for City Council, announcing a bid in March 2021 before withdrawing from the Democratic primary.

His transportation-related work includes the [[Topic/Traffic calming street-width analysis]], which flags neighborhood streets wide enough to be traffic-calming candidates.
