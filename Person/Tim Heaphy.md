## Background

- [UVA Law profile](https://www.law.virginia.edu/faculty/adjunct-profile/tjh9u/1171676): lecturer; former University Counsel for UVA.
- [2018 UVA appointment announcement](https://archive.news.virginia.edu/content/university-counsel-roberts-retire-heaphy-appointed-university-counsel-uva): appointed University Counsel effective September 1, 2018.

## Charlottesville land-use record

- [April 22, 2013 Charlottesville Tomorrow report](https://www.cvilletomorrow.org/meadowbrook-flats-concerns/): called for an external review of [[Project/Meadowbrook Flats]].
- [October 8, 2013 Planning Commission minutes](https://weblink.charlottesville.org/Public/0/edoc/794768/PC_M_10-08-2013.pdf): opposed Meadowbrook Flats over loss of green space and the project's scale and massing.
- [April 8, 2014 Planning Commission minutes](https://weblink.charlottesville.org/Public/0/edoc/795140/PC_A_04-08-2014.pdf): opposed the revised Meadowbrook Flats application as conflicting with the Entrance Corridor Guidelines.
- [Barracks-Emmet project roster](https://www.barracksemmetimprovements.com/about/): represented the Venable Neighborhood Association on the [[Project/Barracks-Emmet intersection improvements]] steering committee.
- [October 30, 2019 steering-committee summary](https://www.barracksemmetimprovements.com/2019/11/07/steering-committee-meeting-summary-2-october-30-2019/): individual comments and votes are not attributed by name, so this source does not establish Heaphy's position on the shared-use path.

The linked [2013 news report](https://www.cvilletomorrow.org/meadowbrook-flats-concerns/), [2013 minutes](https://weblink.charlottesville.org/Public/0/edoc/794768/PC_M_10-08-2013.pdf), and [2014 minutes](https://weblink.charlottesville.org/Public/0/edoc/795140/PC_A_04-08-2014.pdf) document Heaphy's opposition to Meadowbrook Flats but do not identify him as the campaign leader or attribute a project decision to him individually.
