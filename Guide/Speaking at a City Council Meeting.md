A practical guide to addressing Charlottesville City Council during public comment.

**Where & when.** Council meets on an adopted annual schedule (see the city calendar). Meetings stream live via BoxCast, and you can participate virtually through Zoom (register via the city calendar).

**Sign up to speak.** Use the city's [Request to Speak](https://www.charlottesville.gov/692/Request-to-Speak) page to sign up in advance. Written input can also be submitted through the "Matters by the Public" online form.

**The rules.** Public-comment specifics — number of speakers, time per speaker, decorum — are set by Council's Rules and Procedures (Resolution R-24-034, adopted March 18, 2024) and summarized in the city's "Speaking at Council Meeting" brochure. Check those for the current limits before you attend.
