[Charlottesville Code § 15-4(d)](https://library.municode.com/va/charlottesville/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CO_CH15MOVETR_ARTIINGE_S15-4GEPODUMATRENTR) requires at least 14 days of notice and comment for proposed parking regulations lasting at least 30 days. Required notice: city website, affected city agencies, and signs along the affected street. [The city petition form](https://www.charlottesville.gov/DocumentCenter/View/3163/Petition--Parking-Change---PDF) links qualifying parking-change petitions to the same process.

## Notice vs. community engagement

Record this as required notice and comment, separate from any broader community-engagement process. [§ 15-4(d)](https://library.municode.com/va/charlottesville/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CO_CH15MOVETR_ARTIINGE_S15-4GEPODUMATRENTR) does not require neighborhood meetings, open houses, surveys, or iterative design review.
