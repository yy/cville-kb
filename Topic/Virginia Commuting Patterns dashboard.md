The **Virginia Commuting Patterns dashboard** is a Weldon Cooper Center data tool for exploring commuting flows across Virginia and its 133 localities. When checked on July 18, 2026, it used the U.S. Census Bureau's 2022 Longitudinal Employer-Household Dynamics Origin-Destination Employment Statistics data.

## Limitations

The underlying data excludes military personnel, self-employed and informal workers, and secondary and short-term employment. Employer establishment addresses may not reflect where work is performed, and the data cannot distinguish remote, hybrid, and on-site work.
