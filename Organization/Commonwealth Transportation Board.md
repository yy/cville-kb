The **Commonwealth Transportation Board (CTB)** is Virginia's transportation
oversight body — officially, it *"oversees transportation funding, statewide
planning, project selection, and other initiatives in Virginia."* It has 17 members
appointed by the Governor, meets monthly in public session, reviews the Six-Year
Improvement Program, and administers the [[Topic/Smart Scale]] scoring system that
guides which projects receive funding.

The CTB makes the final funding decision on Smart Scale applications, including the
[[Topic/US 29-250 Smart Scale ramps]] now moving through the endorsement process.
