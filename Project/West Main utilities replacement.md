A multi-year, phased project by the Charlottesville Department of Utilities to replace aging gas and water mains along University Avenue, West Main Street, and select side streets. Per the [official project page](https://connect.charlottesville.gov/westmainutilitiesproject) (accessed 2026-08-08):

- **Scope**: replaces about 8,680 feet of cast-iron water mains with ductile iron and about 13,660 feet of cast-iron gas piping with corrosion-resistant high-density polyethylene (HDPE).
- **Purpose**: replace aging, leak-prone mains to improve safety and reliability and reduce service disruptions and maintenance costs.
- **Phase One**: began summer 2026 in the area between the Beta Bridge and the Chancellor Street / University Avenue intersection; estimated construction time four and a half months; replaces gas infrastructure only. Future phases replace gas and water mains simultaneously.
- **Funding**: enterprise funds plus a $7.1 million grant awarded in 2023 by the US DOT Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration under the Natural Gas Distribution Infrastructure Safety and Modernization (NGDISM) program, part of the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act; all gas-main replacement work is supported by this grant.
- **Work hours** (Phase One, per the 7/31/26 update): 9 PM–5 AM, Sunday–Thursday. Recent work covered 2" gas main installation on Rugby Road (substantially complete), Chancellor Street, and Madison Lane.
- **Traffic**: maintenance-of-traffic plans for Phases A–D are posted as PDFs on the project page.
- **Project manager**: Jim Keller, Utilities Senior Project Manager.
- **Status timeline**: scoping, procurement, and Phase I design/planning complete; Phase I construction active.

The corridor is also the subject of the [[Project/West Main-Ridge intersection|West Main / Ridge intersection reconfiguration]] Smart Scale application.
