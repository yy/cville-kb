[[Topic/Smart Scale]] project at Barracks Road and Emmet Street ([Council minutes](https://weblink.charlottesville.org/Public/0/edoc/798802/MINS_20210907Sep07-APPROVED.pdf)). In these records, "SUP" means shared-use path ([agenda packet](https://weblink.charlottesville.org/Public/0/edoc/798580/AGENDA_20210907Sep07.pdf)).

## Scope history

- [March 2, 2020 City Council materials](https://weblink.charlottesville.org/public/0/edoc/794293/AGENDA_20200302Mar02.pdf): original 10-foot shared-use path.
- [Project website](https://www.barracksemmetimprovements.com/about/): steering-committee roster; [[Person/Tim Heaphy|Tim Heaphy]] listed for the Venable Neighborhood Association.
- [October 30, 2019 steering-committee summary](https://www.barracksemmetimprovements.com/2019/11/07/steering-committee-meeting-summary-2-october-30-2019/): four bike/pedestrian options; majority survey preference for a shared-use path; committee comments and votes not attributed by name.
- [September 7, 2021 agenda packet](https://weblink.charlottesville.org/Public/0/edoc/798580/AGENDA_20210907Sep07.pdf): eight-foot functional path width plus two-foot shoulders; "SUP" used for shared-use path.
- [September 7, 2021 approved minutes](https://weblink.charlottesville.org/Public/0/edoc/798802/MINS_20210907Sep07-APPROVED.pdf): Council approved the major design features 5–0.
- [2025 VDOT transportation update](https://www.vdot.virginia.gov/media/vdotvirginiagov/about/districts/2025-culpeper-district-transportation-update_acc.pdf): city eliminated the shared-use path because retaining walls and additional right of way exceeded available funding.
- [August 2025 city update](https://www.charlottesville.gov/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/2259): six-foot sidewalk to Buckingham Road.
- [2026 invitation for bids](https://procurement.opengov.com/portal/charlottesville/projects/221435): approximately 1,500 feet of five-foot sidewalk; procurement closed without a vendor because bids exceeded the project budget.

## Tim Heaphy's documented role

- [Project roster](https://www.barracksemmetimprovements.com/about/): Heaphy was a steering-committee member.
- [Committee summary](https://www.barracksemmetimprovements.com/2019/11/07/steering-committee-meeting-summary-2-october-30-2019/): individual comments and votes are not attributed. This source does not establish which positions were Heaphy's.
