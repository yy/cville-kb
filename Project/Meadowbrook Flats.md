Proposed redevelopment of the Meadowbrook Shopping Center property at 1138 Emmet Street ([city staff report](https://weblink.charlottesville.org/public/0/edoc/795130/Meadowbrook%20Flats.pdf)).

## Opposition and review

- [April 22, 2013 Charlottesville Tomorrow report](https://www.cvilletomorrow.org/meadowbrook-flats-concerns/): initial two-building proposal; about 75 apartments in phase one and 50 apartments plus commercial space in phase two; phase one largely by right and phase two requiring a special use permit. Lori Shinseki identified as creator of an opposition website; [[Person/Tim Heaphy|Tim Heaphy]] called for an external review.
- [October 8, 2013 agenda materials](https://weblink.charlottesville.org/public/0/edoc/795099/PC_A_10-08-2013.pdf): revised 73-unit proposal.
- [October 8, 2013 Planning Commission minutes](https://weblink.charlottesville.org/Public/0/edoc/794768/PC_M_10-08-2013.pdf): Heaphy opposed the loss of green space and the project's scale and massing; Commission recommended denial of the critical-slopes waiver 5–1.
- [January 2014 city staff report](https://weblink.charlottesville.org/public/0/edoc/795130/Meadowbrook%20Flats.pdf): address and property; revised design no longer required a critical-slopes waiver.
- [April 8, 2014 Planning Commission minutes](https://weblink.charlottesville.org/Public/0/edoc/795140/PC_A_04-08-2014.pdf): Heaphy opposed the application as conflicting with the Entrance Corridor Guidelines; applicant requested and received a deferral.
- [Cvillepedia](https://www.cvillepedia.org/Meadowbrook_Flats): says the project was withdrawn but marks that statement "citation needed." Final disposition not verified.

The [April 2013 report](https://www.cvilletomorrow.org/meadowbrook-flats-concerns/), [October 2013 minutes](https://weblink.charlottesville.org/Public/0/edoc/794768/PC_M_10-08-2013.pdf), and [April 2014 minutes](https://weblink.charlottesville.org/Public/0/edoc/795140/PC_A_04-08-2014.pdf) do not name Heaphy as the campaign leader or attribute a project decision to him individually.
