A [[Topic/Smart Scale]] application to reconfigure the West Main Street / Ridge / McIntire intersection — Charlottesville's first Smart Scale application in many years. City Council was set to endorse a resolution of support at its June 15, 2026 meeting.

Per the staff materials, the project *"converts West Water Street and South Street W to one (1)-way streets, creates a pedestrian plaza, includes a dedicated pedestrian and bike signal phase, and incorporates bike lanes, and adds street parking."*

The application is the city's own priority, contrasting with the county/VDOT-driven [[Topic/US 29-250 Smart Scale ramps]] that [[Person/Ben Chambers]] argued the city should not spend its limited Smart Scale capacity on.
