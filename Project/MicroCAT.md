MicroCAT is an on-demand public rideshare service in the Pantops and US 29 corridor areas of Albemarle County. The County's [September 2, 2026 Board actions](https://albemarle.legistar.com/View.ashx?GUID=D505A441-8918-4647-8A2E-E126CC10B2D4&ID=1375273&M=E1) state that Albemarle contracts with Charlottesville Area Transit and Via to provide the service. The [current service page](https://www.charlottesville.gov/1806/MicroCAT) lists fare-free rides Monday through Saturday, 6:30 a.m.–9 p.m., with wheelchair-accessible vehicles and phone booking for riders without smartphones.

## Origin and funding

The [September 2, 2026 County staff report](https://albemarle.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?FullText=1&GUID=26B79E91-8F50-4A79-8195-0E34478355F8&ID=8191236) traces MicroCAT to the 2022 Albemarle County Transit Expansion Plan. Charlottesville Area Transit received a `$1.94 million` state demonstration grant in 2023, Albemarle County committed a `$388,000` local match, and service began in late October 2023. The grant required fare-free service through FY2026.

## 2027 fare schedule

County staff proposed fares beginning in January 2027:

- `$2` per ride for a single passenger;
- `$1` for each additional passenger on the same ride; and
- `$1` per ride for people age 65 or older, people with disabilities, and people with income below 60% of area median income.

Staff said the fares would cover part of the cost and encourage riders to use MicroCAT to supplement fixed-route transit rather than replace it. The adopted FY2027 budget already included the expected fee revenue; staff estimated that not implementing the fares would reduce revenue by up to `$80,000` during the remainder of FY2027.

The County's [official Board actions](https://albemarle.legistar.com/View.ashx?GUID=D505A441-8918-4647-8A2E-E126CC10B2D4&ID=1375273&M=E1) record that the Board of Supervisors adopted the fare resolution 6–0 on September 2, 2026. As of September 20, 2026, the [official meeting page](https://albemarle.legistar.com/MeetingDetail.aspx?GUID=D505A441-8918-4647-8A2E-E126CC10B2D4&ID=1375273&Options=info%7C&Search=) lists the minutes status as final-revised but says published minutes are unavailable.
